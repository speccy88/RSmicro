# Safety and security warning

RSmicro is experimental, not safety-certified, and native simulation is not production control. Emergency stops, overloads, guards, watchdogs, and protective devices must remain independent and safety-rated where required. HMI buttons do not replace physical safety controls; alarm acknowledgement does not reset a hazard; forces can cause unsafe operation. Physical commissioning requires independent verification. Network protocols are unauthenticated and unencrypted and must remain on loopback or isolated trusted networks.
